Gap = campus score minus the score for the same subjects and session type at other campuses; only subjects taught in both periods; 20+ evaluations in each period. Normal variation = twice the usual noise for that many evaluations. Status compares with yesterday's list: New today = listed for the first time; Still on list = also listed yesterday; Removed today = listed yesterday, now within normal variation (not yet a recovery). "Subject most affected" = the subject whose score fell most at that campus (drop × evaluations) and is below other campuses; "was" = that campus's subject average in the previous 14 days; "others" = the same rubric for that subject at other campuses in the last 14 days. Rubric changes use daily rubric scores over the same 14-day windows.
